# ADR-000 — Hackathon execution boundaries

**State:** PROPOSED. Record project-team agreement at kickoff before changing to ACCEPTED.  
**Date:** 2026-10-24

## Decision

1. SettleGraph and BankSim have separate PostgreSQL databases.
2. Three Go programs in one repository: API, worker and simulated financial provider.
3. Two fictional banks are represented inside one BankSim service, not as two extra microservices.
4. Transactions have ETB `int64` minor units; incoming/outgoing JSON money uses decimal strings.
5. Reconciliation has separate `ORDER_PAYMENT` and `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT` stages, snapshot/proof rules, and source watermark checks.
6. AI is advisory and evidence-gated; only authorized humans resolve cases.
7. Next.js and Flutter consume the same public OpenAPI, not BankSim internal endpoints.
8. The build always supports a deterministic local/offline demonstration and must never imply real bank integration.

## Consequences

A compact solo-backend architecture that remains realistic to test; some advanced product features and independent scaling are deferred. The implementation must be truthful about simulation and verification limits.
